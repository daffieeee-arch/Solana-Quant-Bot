## Operational clock semantics

Acquisition `acquired_at.wall_ms` records receipt acquisition time after response, not a request-start/finish interval. Original receipt source/range identity and payload byte hashes are checked; duplicate receipt identities or overlapping ranges from the same object are refused. Selected payload bytes exclude metadata, retries, protocol overhead and physical duplicate storage.

The existing decoder `processed_at_unix_ms` / `finished_at_unix_ms` are UTC wall clocks around its bounded processing/report phase, not a download or full process elapsed counter. They remain strings, may be affected by wall-clock correction and are not used to assert a monotone runtime. Missing clocks stay unavailable. Report generation separately shows its original UTC start/end and recorded `perf_counter` duration **8.770708198018838 seconds**. This is the old pilot report generation, not current browser generation or Rust decode time. These operational fields never enter historical plots, chain ordering, replay or trading statistics.
